the candidate applies on the careers site, which sends the resume to the recruiter. if the resume matches the role the recruiter books a phone screen with the hiring manager, otherwise it sends a rejection email. if the panel approves after the onsite, HR makes an offer, else the hiring manager sends it back to the recruiter. there is a background check in front of HR.
the patient checks in at reception, which pulls the record from the EHR. the nurse takes vitals and sends them to the doctor. if the doctor needs tests the lab runs bloodwork, otherwise the doctor sends a prescription to the pharmacy. the pharmacy bills insurance, falling back to the patient when insurance denies the claim.
the seller ships the parcel to the depot, which sorts it and hands it to the courier. the courier delivers to the customer only if someone is home, else it leaves it at the locker. customs inspects international parcels before the depot. the depot has several sorting lines that take parcels round robin.
the student submits the assignment to the LMS, which runs a plagiarism check. if the check flags it the teacher reviews it with the student, otherwise the TA grades it. the TA posts the grade to the gradebook, and if the grade is failing the advisor emails the student.
the client signs the engagement letter and the paralegal opens a matter in the case system. if there is a conflict of interest the partner declines the case, else the associate drafts the motion. the partner reviews the draft and sends it back to the associate when changes are needed. the clerk files the motion with the court.
the supplier delivers steel to receiving, which logs it in the ERP. the press stamps the parts and the robot welds them. if a weld fails inspection the part goes to rework, otherwise it moves to painting. rework sends fixed parts back to inspection. there are multiple presses using least connections.
the user uploads a video through the web app to the API, which stores it in S3 and pushes a job to the queue. the transcoder pulls from the queue and writes renditions to the CDN, retrying the job when the transcoder crashes. if the video is longer than an hour the API notifies the user by email, otherwise it sends a push notification. the CDN is in front of the player and the transcoder is multiple with round robin. there's a rate limiter infront of the API.
